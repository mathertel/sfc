Effective Implementation for Storytelling

Demonstrate a minimal approach to implement storytelling and creating storytelling effects on the web using modern browser technologies best suited to the task.

Storytelling is still important

Storytelling is still a good approach to tell, demonstrate or teach using well-sized chunks of information and include movie style visuals, animations, illustrations or even audio next to text based content. Visual storytelling is the best way to stand out and grab the attention of users.

To fit into the economics of the internet the text part of any page or article should not be underestimated. This is the content that gets indexed and highlighted when searching and this is the information that AI agents can understand easily.

Here I assemble the techniques for an effective storytelling on the web with a minimal set of techniques and tools by leveraging the capabilities of modern web technologies, especially CSS.

Storytelling done right from the start

As a general approach I recommend what also is best practice in many frameworks:

  • Breaking down the story into discrete steps, each represented by a section or container element.
  • These steps should be visually distinct and can be enhanced with animations or transitions that are triggered as the user scrolls through the story.
  • Trigger animations or transitions when a story step enters (or leaves) the viewport.
  • Annotate the html container objects with appropriate classes or data attributes to indicate their role in the story.

This ensures that the user focuses on one piece of content at a time, creating a more engaging and immersive storytelling experience.

In the age of AI not only humans are reading so I propose to stick to the foillowing rules as well:

  • The steps should use text as the main content, complemented by visuals, animations, or other media to enhance the storytelling experience.
  • visuals like pictures or illustrations should complement the text content only but bring no additional meaningfull information.
